# Product brief

Last updated: 2026-10-28 (saasaloy-setup)

- **Name**: GadgetRow
- **Language**: English (`en`)
- **Website**: https://gadgetrow.com

## Project scope

This repository contains the landing website for GadgetRow, a physical shop that sells laptops, computers, and accessories in Bangladesh. The website serves customers across Bangladesh. Social media marketing is a separate business activity, and the website provides product pages that the shop can share.

The website helps customers find products and contact the shop through WhatsApp. The default purchase flow includes a shop visit so customers can inspect the product before they buy. The website does not accept orders or payments. International sales and listings from other shops are outside the scope.

## Audience

Students who need a laptop for study within a fixed budget, and professionals who need a laptop or computer for work.

## Problem

Customers need help to choose a computer that fits their needs and budget. They want product details and advice before a shop visit. They also want to inspect the product before they pay.

## Current alternative

Customers compare product listings on Facebook and ask sellers through messages. They also visit computer shops and ask staff for advice.

## Differentiator

GadgetRow helps customers choose a computer for their work or study budget through WhatsApp before they visit the shop. Customers can then inspect the product at the physical shop before they buy.
