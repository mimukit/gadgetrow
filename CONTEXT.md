# GadgetRow

GadgetRow is a computer retailer with a physical shop in Bangladesh. Its online store helps customers find products and contact the shop before buying.

## Overview

### What it is

A product website for GadgetRow’s laptops, computers, and accessories. Customers contact the shop through WhatsApp to discuss a purchase.

### Who it is for

Customers across Bangladesh who want to buy laptops, computers, or accessories. The default purchase flow serves customers who visit the shop to inspect products before buying.

### The problem it solves

Customers need a way to find GadgetRow’s products and contact the shop before they visit or buy.

### Core capabilities

- Show products and their details.
- Help customers send product enquiries through WhatsApp.
- Help customers find and visit the physical shop.
- Provide product pages that GadgetRow can share on social media.

### Non-goals

- Accept orders or payments through the website.
- Sell internationally.
- List products from other shops.

## Store terms

### Product

A laptop, computer, or accessory that GadgetRow offers for sale.

### Online store

GadgetRow’s website for product discovery and customer enquiries. It does not accept orders or payments.
_Avoid: marketplace_

### Physical shop

GadgetRow’s retail premises, where customers can inspect products before buying.
_Avoid: showroom_

### Customer enquiry

A customer’s message to GadgetRow through WhatsApp about a product or possible purchase. An enquiry is not a confirmed order.
_Avoid: order_

### Shop visit

A customer’s visit to the physical shop to inspect a product and discuss or complete a purchase.
